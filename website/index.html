For Twitch streamers using OBS Studio

Run your whole stream without leaving your game.

Switch scenes, mute your mic, read chat and go live from one window, or with keyboard shortcuts while your game keeps focus. No more alt-tabbing out of the match.

CtrlShiftF11×2

Go live or end your stream. Two presses, so a stray key never starts a broadcast.

The controls you reach for mid-stream, in one place

Stream Command connects to OBS on your PC and to your Twitch account. Everything below works from one window, and the essentials work from your keyboard while you play.

Go live without leaving your game

Start and stop your stream, switch scenes and manage audio from one screen, with a live preview of what viewers actually see right next to your controls.

  • See at a glance whether OBS is connected
  • Scene buttons labelled with their shortcut keys
  • Live preview through OBS Virtual Camera

Switch scenes mid-match

Global shortcuts work while your game has focus. Your game never loses the window, and a small on-screen note confirms the scene that is now live.

Read and reply to chat

Your Twitch chat in a panel beside your controls, with alerts for follows, subs and raids. Type a reply and send it without switching windows.

Thank every follower automatically

Automation rules respond to follows, subs, gift subs and raids, so your community gets acknowledged even when you are mid-fight.

And the rest of your streaming routine

Pre-stream checklist

Run your own setup list before going live, so you stop discovering a muted mic twenty minutes in.

Stream planner

Keep upcoming streams in one place and see what is next.

Opens OBS for you

If OBS is not running, Stream Command starts it minimised and connects automatically. One app to open, nothing covering your game.

On-stream alerts

Show follower and subscriber alerts on your broadcast as they happen.

Stream summary

Session length and how much your automation handled, every time you end a stream.

Make it yours

Pick an accent colour and the whole app restyles around it. Change or turn off the shortcut keys in Settings.

Coming in the next update

Clips: your best moments, cut into vertical videos

Stream Command will record your stream through OBS and turn the highlights into 1080×1920 clips when you stop, with your facecam placed above the gameplay. Clip the last few seconds with Ctrl Shift F9, let your mods type !clip in chat, or let it spot chat going wild for you.

What you need

To get started

  • Windows 10 version 2004 or newer, or Windows 11
  • OBS Studio 28 or newer, with its WebSocket server turned on (in OBS: Tools, then WebSocket Server Settings)
  • A Twitch account, to connect chat and alerts

Your data stays on your PC

Stream Command talks to Twitch and to your own copy of OBS, and nothing else. There is no account to create and no server of ours in the middle. Your Twitch sign-in and OBS password are encrypted and stored only on your machine. Read the privacy policy.

Keep your eyes on the game.

Install Stream Command from the Microsoft Store and connect OBS and Twitch in a couple of minutes. The in-app Setup Guide walks you through it.
