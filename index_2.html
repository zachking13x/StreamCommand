Setup

Up in 2 minutes.

01

Download from the Microsoft Store

Free to install on Windows 10 or 11. No account to create — just download and open.

02

Connect Twitch in the setup wizard

Get a free OAuth token at twitchapps.com/tmi, paste it in — your live chat appears instantly.

03

Link OBS (optional, 30 seconds)

Enable WebSocket Server in OBS → Tools menu. Enter the port in Stream Command. Done — full OBS control unlocked.

04

Stream your way

Everything is stored locally on your device. No cloud accounts, no subscriptions required to start.
